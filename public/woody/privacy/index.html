Privacy policy

Updated November 2026

This covers the Woody page and its waiting list. The rest of wojciech.io has its own privacy policy.

Who is responsible

I am Wojciech Luszczynski, a private individual, and I am responsible for your data. My postal address is in the policy of wojciech.io. Write to me at hello@wojciech.io.

Visiting the page

The page sets no cookies. Your browser keeps one thing: that you closed the cookie note. Cloudflare serves the page and counts visits with a small script. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. Cloudflare adds that script itself, so this page cannot switch it off, but you can block static.cloudflareinsights.com.

The waiting list

If you join, I keep your email address, the time you agreed and the time you confirmed it. I use them to send the confirmation and one email on the day Woody opens, nothing else. I share your address only with the services below, and I rely on your consent.

A sign up you do not confirm is deleted after 24 hours. To stop abuse, the form briefly counts requests by IP address (10 minutes) and by a scrambled email address (one hour).

Who handles the data

Resend sends my emails. Cloudflare hosts the page and stores the list. Both are US companies, so your data may be processed outside the EU.

How long I keep it

Your address stays on the list until you ask me to remove it.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold and ask for a copy. You can have it corrected, deleted or restricted. You can withdraw your consent at any time, which does not undo what happened before. Write to hello@wojciech.io to do any of this or to leave the list. You can also complain to your data protection authority.
