Problem

More registrations can look like growth even when the booking rate or repeat behaviour gets worse. Recent cohorts have also had less time to come back.

Solution

Cohorts you can trace

A browser workbench that compares eligible cohorts, traces the booking steps and keeps service-quality indicators apart from acquisition activity.

Implementation

Compare
Eligible cohorts, side by side.
Trace
The booking steps, down to the counts.
Explain
Each denominator comes with its rule.

This began as a take-home exercise. A browser test run is recorded for the build.

Benefits

Each finding points to the counts behind it.

A working analytical prototype on prepared aggregate data, not a live account.
