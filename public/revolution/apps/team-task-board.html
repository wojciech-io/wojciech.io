        01 Problem

        Action items lived in notes and spreadsheets. Nobody could say who owned what, or where follow-up had stalled.

        02 Solution

        One board,owners and dates

        A shared Kanban with owners, due dates and reminders. I added an export, so the existing reporting routine kept working.

        03 Implementation

        How itis built

        A small Python app. Cards sit in SQLite. A card moves across the board and keeps its owner and its deadline. Due items come up for follow-up. A spreadsheet export carries the fields that reporting needs outside the app.

        • Python
        • SQLite
        • Spreadsheet export
