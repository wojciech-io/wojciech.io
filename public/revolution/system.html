Preview · design system

Library

Everything the preview is made of, in one place: tokens read live from the CSS, type, icons, components, screens, covers, motion, both modes and the ten page templates.

Tokens

Read live from the stylesheets this preview ships (g22.css, v4.css, motion.css), so this tab cannot drift from the code: … custom properties found. Dark or default is the :root value, Light is the html[data-mode="light"] override. Rule: one accent, olive #4D5C00 on light, lime #D2FF00 on dark.

Rules that do not change

12 / 14 / 18 / 28 / 56 px, plus 120 px for display on desktop.

Line

Tap target

44 px for every control on a phone. Text never below 12 px.

Spacing

Page margin 17 px on v4 pages; one section step --sp (11vw) above and below every section.

B: v4 pages (19 pages)

Apps, Insights notes and Work cases. Mona Sans 700, uppercase, letter spacing -.038em, normal width. Playfair appears only as the second line of a display heading, the last phrase of a statement and the first line of a big number.

Body and code

Screens

35 application screens in light windows, assets/real/ui-*.webp. Rule: an interface is always shown whole, with all four corners; on phones a portrait variant (16 files ending -m) replaces the full screen. Data is anonymised example data.

Covers and photos

Rules for frames and backgrounds. These come from the decisions already taken for the preview, not from new taste.

Cover kinds

A note cover is a mark of the model, a screen of an application, or a photo of the role. Never text inside the image.

Screens are whole

Never cropped by a section or a screen edge. On phones the -m portrait replaces the desktop screen.

Photos

Only approved photos of Wojciech and the people on the testimonials. No generated photos of him; the cyborg appears only as the agent (Home, About).

Background

Flat v4 covers have no generated hero photo and no contour lines (mono.css). Frames use radius 12 and a 1 px line.

Ratios in use

16:9 lens, 4:3 plate, 7:5 menu, 32:21 screen, 1200 × 630 Open Graph, square 120 px avatar.

Samples

Motion

One easing, one distance, one duration (motion.css): --m-ease: cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.1,1), --m-dist: 22px, --m-dur: .8s, --m-step: .08s. Everything moves with transform, translate, scale or opacity, so nothing reflows. With prefers-reduced-motion or without JS the page is fully visible and the demos jump to the end state.

Hooks

data-m="rise"

Section rises 22 px and fades in.

data-m="stagger"

Children rise one after another, 80 ms apart (max 8 steps).

data-m="bar" / "barv"

Bars draw once from the left or from the bottom.

data-m="count"

Numbers count up once.

Modes

The same components, dark and light, side by side regardless of the page mode. Dark: olive ink, paper text, lime accent. Light: cream, olive text, olive accent #4D5C00. The footer card stays dark in both modes today; another thread owns the decision on what should follow the mode.
