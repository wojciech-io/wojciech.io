Problem

Website enquiries came through forms with different fields, confirmations and routing needs. Tracing a submission to the right sales record was hard.

Solution

One pattern, every form

A shared submission pattern that connects the form, the confirmation message, the CRM fields and the measurement event.

Implementation

Map
Form fields go to the matching CRM fields.
Route
The routing rule picks the owner.
Record
Every submission leaves a measurement event.

Each form has its own validation list.

Benefits

Every form follows the same path into the CRM. Exceptions are reviewed one form at a time.
