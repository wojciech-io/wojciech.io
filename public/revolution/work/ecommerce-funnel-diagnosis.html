01 Problem

Traffic and product interest were visible. The team needed to see where shoppers stopped between reading a product page and placing an order.

02 Solution

The step wortha test

I used funnel and acquisition reports to find the steps that deserved a closer look, and to separate product interest from checkout behaviour.

03 Implementation

How itis built

The analysis follows product views, basket activity and checkout steps. It compares product families one by one and keeps search visibility beside the funnel as context.

04 Benefits

The analysis flagged the move from product page to basket and gave the team a concrete place to test.
