01 Problem

The team needed to turn onboarding activity, usage changes and billing signals into useful outreach without overloading customers or sales.

02 Solution

Scenarios withan owner

I designed lifecycle scenarios. Each has an owner, an outcome and conditions for automated or human action.

03 Implementation

How itis built

The design maps a signal to an eligibility check, a proposed intervention and a measured outcome. Sensitive or unclear cases need a person to approve the exact action. The handoff has a decision map and scenario playbooks.

  • Lifecycle orchestration
  • Event triggers
  • Approval gates

04 Benefits

A pilotspecification

A team can discuss overlap, ownership and expected outcomes before building any flow.
