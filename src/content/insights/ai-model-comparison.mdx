This page is the one I keep open when someone asks me which model to use. It lists every model I currently compare, with the list price, the cache price, the long-context rule, the independent score and what a full benchmark run actually cost.

Numbers were read on 16 September 2026 against Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3. I update the page within 72 hours of a new index version or a major launch.

## The short answer

- **Best overall:** Claude Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra, tied at the top. Astra cost 59% less to run the index; Fable 5.1 is cheaper on long prompts and on cache.
- **Best value near the top:** Claude Opus 5, two to three points below the leaders at half their price, and Muse Spark 1.3, level with GPT-5.6 Sol at under a third of Sol's list price.
- **Fastest:** Gemini 3.8 Flash, at about 332 tokens per second.
- **Cheapest that is still capable:** GPT-5.6 Luna, and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash if you can run work off-peak.
- **Long prompts:** Anthropic's models, which keep one rate across 1M tokens while OpenAI and xAI surcharge long requests.

## Price is not the bill

The run-cost column is the one I would read first. It is what Artificial Analysis actually paid to put each model through the same set of tasks, so it captures how many tokens a model spends on the way, including reasoning.

Three examples from the table:

- **Sonnet 5 against Luna.** Level on the index, within a point. The index run cost $6,998 on Sonnet 5 and $320 on Luna, 22 times as much, because Sonnet 5 wrote 370M tokens to Luna's 150M. Details in [Luna against Sonnet 5](/insights/gpt-5-6-vs-claude-5/).
- **Astra against Opus 5.** Astra lists at twice Opus 5's price and cost 27% less to run the index. Details in [Astra against Opus 5](/insights/gpt-6-astra-vs-claude-opus-5/).
- **Terra against Sonnet 5.** Sonnet 5 is cheaper per output token and cost 2.8 times as much to run the index. Details in [Terra against Sonnet 5](/insights/claude-sonnet-5-vs-gpt-5-6-terra/).

<KeyPoint>
  Price per token tells you what a token costs. Run cost tells you what a finished job costs. The two disagree often enough that picking a model by its price list is a coin toss.
</KeyPoint>

## How this table is built

Every score comes from one version of one index, Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.3, because scores from different versions sit on different scales. Every price comes from the vendor's own pricing page, and every figure has a source and the date it was read. Where a number is not published or not comparable, the cell says so instead of guessing.

Speeds are live measurements and move from day to day, so I round them. Run costs are what Artificial Analysis paid to run its own index. They are not your workload, but they are the fairest single comparison of cost per finished task I know of.

## Questions people asked

### What is the best AI model right now?

On Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index v4.3, read on 16 September 2026, Claude Fable 5.1 scores 53.37 and GPT-6 Astra 52.81, which Artificial Analysis treats as a tie for first. Claude Opus 5 follows at 50.70. Both leaders list at $10 per million input tokens and $50 output, but Astra cost about 59% less to run the full index because it writes far fewer tokens. For prompts above 272K tokens Fable 5.1 is cheaper, because OpenAI doubles Astra's input price past that line.

### What is the cheapest good AI model?

GPT-5.6 Luna lists at $0.20 per million input tokens and $1.20 output and scores 37.50 on the Intelligence Index v4.3, within a point of Claude Sonnet 5, which costs ten times as much on input. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash scores 39.55 and costs $0.15 / $0.60 off-peak, but $0.30 / $1.20 during weekday peak hours, which cover the European working morning.

### What is the fastest AI model?

Gemini 3.8 Flash, at about 332 output tokens per second on Artificial Analysis's measurements, followed by Muse Spark 1.3 at about 235 and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at about 218. The frontier models are much slower: Claude Fable 5.1 at about 65, GPT-6 Astra at about 53 and Claude Opus 5 at about 50.

### Which AI model is best for very long prompts?

Anthropic bills its models at one rate across the full 1M-token window, while OpenAI charges 2x input and 1.5x output for the whole request once a prompt passes 272K input tokens, and Grok 4.6 doubles every rate from 200K and stops at 500K. For work that regularly crosses those lines, Claude Opus 5 or Fable 5.1 is usually the cheaper choice at the top, and Luna stays cheap even after its surcharge.

### How often is this comparison updated?

Within 72 hours of a new Artificial Analysis index version or a major model launch, and at least monthly. Every number carries its source and the date it was read, and scores from different index versions are never mixed in one table.
